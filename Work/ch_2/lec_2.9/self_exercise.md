# Self Exercise - 2.9

## Q1. What is bias in machine learning?

**Answer:** Bias is the error introduced by simplifying assumptions in a machine learning model. High bias can cause a model to miss important patterns, leading to underfitting.

---

## Q2. What is variance in machine learning?

**Answer:** Variance refers to how much a model's predictions change when trained on different datasets. High variance can cause a model to fit noise instead of actual patterns, leading to overfitting.

---

## Q3. Explain the concept of the bias-variance trade-off.

**Answer:** The bias-variance trade-off is the balance between minimizing bias and minimizing variance. Lower bias often increases variance, while lower variance can increase bias. The goal is to find an optimal balance.

---

## Q4. How does increasing model complexity affect bias and variance?

**Answer:** Increasing model complexity generally decreases bias because the model can learn more patterns, but it often increases variance because the model becomes sensitive to noise.

---

## Q5. What is underfitting, and when does it occur?

**Answer:** Underfitting occurs when a model is too simple to capture the underlying patterns in the data. It typically occurs when the model has high bias and low complexity.

---

## Q6. What are the common causes of underfitting in a model?

**Answer:** Common causes include insufficient model complexity, too few features, excessive regularization, and inadequate training time.

---

## Q7. What is overfitting, and why is it a problem?

**Answer:** Overfitting occurs when a model learns both patterns and noise in training data, causing poor performance on unseen data.

---

## Q8. What are the common causes of overfitting?

**Answer:** Common causes include excessively complex models, small datasets, too many features, and insufficient regularization.

---

## Q9. How can you identify overfitting and underfitting using training and testing performance?

**Answer:** Underfitting occurs when both training and testing accuracy are low. Overfitting occurs when training accuracy is high but testing accuracy is significantly lower.

---

## Q10. What techniques can be used to reduce overfitting?

**Answer:** Techniques include cross-validation, regularization, dropout, pruning, early stopping, increasing dataset size, and feature selection.

---

## Q11. How does regularization help in balancing bias and variance?

**Answer:** Regularization adds penalties to model complexity, reducing variance and preventing overfitting while slightly increasing bias.

---

## Q12. Why is achieving the right balance between bias and variance important for model performance?

**Answer:** Achieving the right balance ensures that the model generalizes well to unseen data, improving prediction accuracy and robustness.
